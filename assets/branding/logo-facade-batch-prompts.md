# Variações de logo — fachada financeira + IA

Geradas separadamente com a ferramenta integrada `image_gen` em 2026-09-09.

## Opção 1 — Digital Temple

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: polished logo concept for a modern AI financial-research software product.
Brand name: render exactly once and spell exactly "Assistente de Research Financeiro".
Core concept: combine an abstract bank façade, a financial bar chart, columns constructed from numerical data, and a magnifying glass in the center. The architectural columns ARE the chart bars: different heights but aligned on one base. Each bar is visibly built from crisp rows or blocks of digits 0–9, as if structured financial data forms the architecture. The magnifying glass overlaps the central bars and reveals or analyzes the numbers. Integrate everything into one coherent emblem.
Brand character: intelligent, precise, auditable, technical, contemporary; visibly an AI/data product rather than a law firm or traditional private bank.
Composition: one centered finished logo lockup on a square canvas; emblem above, wordmark below; generous margin and balanced negative space.
Typography: clean modern geometric sans-serif only; "ASSISTENTE DE" small and tracked, "RESEARCH FINANCEIRO" larger on two balanced lines; excellent kerning.
Rendering: crisp flat vector-like artwork, limited palette, strong silhouette, usable at reduced size.
Constraints: one finished design only; no mockup, no presentation board, no slogan, no bank names, no watermark, no classical serif lettering, no gold, no marble, no crest, no scales of justice, no gavel, no shield, no coin, no dollar sign, no bull, no robot, no literal brain, no stock arrow, no generic circuit-board background, no glow cloud, no 3D, no photorealism.
Variation 1 — DIGITAL TEMPLE.
Build a compact angular façade using five rectangular data-bar columns under a single thin, forward-leaning roof line. The five bar heights form a clear analytical histogram while still reading as a bank façade. Build each bar from neatly stacked tiny digital numerals with consistent spacing. Place a bold circular magnifying lens over the middle three bars; inside the lens, enlarge a short cluster of digits and connect three of them with subtle node lines to imply AI pattern recognition. Palette: electric cyan and cobalt blue with near-white details on deep navy. Flat solid colors, no gradients.
```
